Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk, VA-NC tunnel car wash market

Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk, VA-NC has 83 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 2 tunnels reported in development, for about 1,802,655 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 21,719 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: fewer people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is denser than average.

Even so, its whitespace index is 21.0 (estimate), from 123 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 41 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 8 of 182 in the Southeast region. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 39; at 25,000 it ranks 40.

About these numbers

Whitespace index is an estimate: the weighted population in tracts short of tunnels, divided by 20,000, with dense tracts weighted down and a small income adjustment. It ranks markets to study, not buildable sites. Tunnel counts (verified October 2026) and population (Esri 2024) are actual. Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table; the full method is on Method and FAQ.
