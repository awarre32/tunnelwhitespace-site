Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL tunnel car wash market

Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL has 166 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 29 tunnels reported in development, for about 6,248,398 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 37,641 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: more people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is thinner than average.

Its whitespace index is 168.7 (estimate), from 1,012 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 3 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 1 of 182 in the Southeast region. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 3; at 25,000 it ranks 4.

About these numbers

Whitespace index is an estimate: the weighted population in tracts short of tunnels, divided by 20,000, with dense tracts weighted down and a small income adjustment. It ranks markets to study, not buildable sites. Tunnel counts (verified October 2026) and population (Esri 2024) are actual. Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table; the full method is on Method and FAQ.
