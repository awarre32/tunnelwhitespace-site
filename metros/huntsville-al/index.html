Huntsville, AL tunnel car wash market

Huntsville, AL has 35 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 0 reported in development for about 531,872 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 15,196 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: fewer people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is denser than average.

Even so, its whitespace index is 1.0 (estimate), from 4 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 198 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 41 of 182 in the Southeast. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 241; at 25,000 it ranks 202.

About these numbers

Whitespace index (estimate): the weighted population in a metro's tracts that are short of tunnel car washes, divided by 20,000. Dense urban tracts are weighted down and income nudges demand. It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites. Tunnels (operating only; tunnels in development are their own column and count as open in the index) and population are actual counts (tunnels verified October 2026, population Esri 2024 estimates). Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table.

How the gap is measured, and what the map does not show, is on Method and FAQ.
