Fargo, ND-MN tunnel car wash market

Fargo, ND-MN has 17 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 1 tunnel reported in development, for about 266,262 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 15,662 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: fewer people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is denser than average.

Its whitespace index is 0.8 (estimate), from 4 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 217 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 54 of 284 in the Midwest region. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 264; at 25,000 it ranks 174.

About these numbers

Whitespace index is an estimate: the weighted population in tracts short of tunnels, divided by 20,000, with dense tracts weighted down and a small income adjustment. It ranks markets to study, not buildable sites. Tunnel counts (verified October 2026) and population (Esri 2024) are actual. Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table; the full method is on Method and FAQ.
