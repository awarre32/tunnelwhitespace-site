Kingsport-Bristol, TN-VA tunnel car wash market

Kingsport-Bristol, TN-VA has 16 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and none reported in development, for about 310,262 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 19,391 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: fewer people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is denser than average.

No census tract here is flagged as short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people, so its whitespace index is 0, which ranks 316 (tied) of 925. A tract is rated only when at least 20,000 people live within its 10-minute drive, and flagged when that population could support at least one more tunnel than it has. Its population is spread thin: most tracts have fewer than 20,000 people within a 10-minute drive.

About these numbers

Whitespace index is an estimate: the weighted population in tracts short of tunnels, divided by 20,000, with dense tracts weighted down and a small income adjustment. It ranks markets to study, not buildable sites. Tunnel counts (verified October 2026) and population (Esri 2024) are actual. Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table; the full method is on Method and FAQ.
