Jackson, TN tunnel car wash market

Jackson, TN has 8 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 1 tunnel reported in development, for about 181,471 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 22,684 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: fewer people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is denser than average.

Its whitespace index is 0.7 (estimate), from 5 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 225 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 50 of 182 in the Southeast region. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 281; at 25,000 it ranks 253 (tied).

About these numbers

Whitespace index is an estimate: the weighted population in tracts short of tunnels, divided by 20,000, with dense tracts weighted down and a small income adjustment. It ranks markets to study, not buildable sites. Tunnel counts (verified October 2026) and population (Esri 2024) are actual. Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table; the full method is on Method and FAQ.
