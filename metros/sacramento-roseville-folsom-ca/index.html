Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA tunnel car wash market

Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA has 76 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 1 tunnel reported in development, for about 2,466,648 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 32,456 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: close to the figure for the U.S. as a whole.

Its whitespace index is 61.7 (estimate), from 310 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 18 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 7 of 83 in the Pacific region. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 20; at 25,000 it ranks 18.

About these numbers

Whitespace index is an estimate: the weighted population in tracts short of tunnels, divided by 20,000, with dense tracts weighted down and a small income adjustment. It ranks markets to study, not buildable sites. Tunnel counts (verified October 2026) and population (Esri 2024) are actual. Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table; the full method is on Method and FAQ.
