Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario, CA tunnel car wash market

Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario, CA has 200 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 6 reported in development for about 4,719,812 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 23,599 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: fewer people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is denser than average.

Even so, its whitespace index is 71.0 (estimate), from 284 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 16 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 6 of 83 in the Pacific. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 15; at 25,000 it ranks 16.

About these numbers

Whitespace index (estimate): the weighted population in a metro's tracts that are short of tunnel car washes, divided by 20,000. Dense urban tracts are weighted down and income nudges demand. It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites. Tunnels (operating only; tunnels in development are their own column and count as open in the index) and population are actual counts (tunnels verified October 2026, population Esri 2024 estimates). Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table.

How the gap is measured, and what the map does not show, is on Method and FAQ.
