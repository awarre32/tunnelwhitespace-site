Des Moines-West Des Moines, IA tunnel car wash market

Des Moines-West Des Moines, IA has 45 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 0 reported in development for about 751,652 people (Esri 2024 estimate). That is about 16,703 people per operating tunnel against about 28,400 nationally: fewer people per tunnel than the U.S. as a whole, so supply is denser than average.

Even so, its whitespace index is 3.2 (estimate), from 16 census tracts that look short of tunnels at one per 20,000 people. That ranks 121 of 925 U.S. metro and micro areas and 25 of 284 in the Midwest. At 15,000 people per tunnel it ranks 104; at 25,000 it ranks 113.

About these numbers

Whitespace index (estimate): the weighted population in a metro's tracts that are short of tunnel car washes, divided by 20,000. Dense urban tracts are weighted down and income nudges demand. It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites. Tunnels (operating only; tunnels in development are their own column and count as open in the index) and population are actual counts (tunnels verified October 2026, population Esri 2024 estimates). Rankings at 25,000 and 15,000 people per tunnel are in the map's market table.
