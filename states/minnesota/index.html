Tunnel car washes in Minnesota

The map counts 126 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Minnesota and 3 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 32 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Minnesota, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington, MN-WI, La Crosse-Onalaska, WI-MN, Duluth, MN-WI, Mankato, MN and Rochester, MN.

It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites.
