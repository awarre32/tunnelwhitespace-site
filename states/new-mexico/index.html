Tunnel car washes in New Mexico

The map counts 86 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in New Mexico and 1 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 36 (tied) of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of New Mexico, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Albuquerque, NM, Santa Fe, NM and Las Cruces, NM.

Whitespace index (estimate): the weighted population in a metro's tracts that are short of tunnel car washes, divided by 20,000. Dense urban tracts are weighted down and income nudges demand. It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites.
