Tunnel car washes in Massachusetts

The map counts 216 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Massachusetts and 1 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 20 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Massachusetts, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Boston-Cambridge-Newton, MA-NH, Providence-Warwick, RI-MA, Worcester, MA, Springfield, MA and Barnstable Town, MA.
