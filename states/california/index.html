Tunnel car washes in California

The map counts 972 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in California and 33 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 2 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the California metros with the most room are Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA, San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA, San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad, CA, Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario, CA and Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA.
