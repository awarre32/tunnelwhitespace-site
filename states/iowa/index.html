Tunnel car washes in Iowa

The map counts 137 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Iowa and none reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 30 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Iowa, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Omaha, NE-IA, Des Moines-West Des Moines, IA, Davenport-Moline-Rock Island, IA-IL, Cedar Rapids, IA and Waterloo-Cedar Falls, IA.
