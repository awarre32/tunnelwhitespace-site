Tunnel car washes in North Carolina

The map counts 435 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in North Carolina and 5 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 7 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the North Carolina metros with the most room are Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia, NC-SC, Raleigh-Cary, NC, Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk, VA-NC, Durham-Chapel Hill, NC and Winston-Salem, NC.
