Tunnel car washes in Oklahoma

The map counts 200 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Oklahoma and 6 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 22 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the Oklahoma metros with the most room are Tulsa, OK, Oklahoma City, OK, Fort Smith, AR-OK and Shawnee, OK (micro).
