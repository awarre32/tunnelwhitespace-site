Tunnel car washes in New York

The map counts 339 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in New York and 7 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 9 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of New York, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are New York-Newark-Jersey City, NY-NJ, Buffalo-Cheektowaga, NY, Albany-Schenectady-Troy, NY, Rochester, NY and Syracuse, NY.
