Tunnel car washes in Ohio

The map counts 451 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Ohio and 3 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 6 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Ohio, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Cincinnati, OH-KY-IN, Cleveland, OH, Columbus, OH, Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek, OH and Akron, OH.
