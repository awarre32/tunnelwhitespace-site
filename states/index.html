Tunnel car washes by state

The map counts 11,854 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) and 347 reported in development across the U.S. The five states with the most are Texas (1,534), California (972), Florida (932), Georgia (501) and Illinois (470). Updated November 25, 2026.
