Tunnel car washes in Pennsylvania

The map counts 322 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Pennsylvania and 16 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 11 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Pennsylvania, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington, PA-NJ-DE-MD, Pittsburgh, PA, Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton, PA-NJ, Lancaster, PA and Erie, PA.
