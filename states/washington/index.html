Tunnel car washes in Washington

The map counts 163 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Washington and 3 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 26 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Washington, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Seattle-Tacoma-Bellevue, WA, Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro, OR-WA, Olympia-Lacey-Tumwater, WA, Spokane-Spokane Valley, WA and Bellingham, WA.

Whitespace index (estimate): the weighted population in a metro's tracts that are short of tunnel car washes, divided by 20,000. Dense urban tracts are weighted down and income nudges demand. It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites.
