Tunnel car washes in Arkansas

The map counts 135 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Arkansas and 7 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 31 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the Arkansas metros with the most room are Memphis, TN-MS-AR, Little Rock-North Little Rock-Conway, AR, Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers, AR, Fort Smith, AR-OK and Texarkana, TX-AR.
