Tunnel car washes in Missouri

The map counts 246 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Missouri and 3 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 17 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Missouri, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are St. Louis, MO-IL, Kansas City, MO-KS, Columbia, MO, Springfield, MO and Quincy, IL-MO (micro).
