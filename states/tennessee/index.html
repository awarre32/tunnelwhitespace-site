Tunnel car washes in Tennessee

The map counts 336 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Tennessee and 4 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 10 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the Tennessee metros with the most room are Nashville-Davidson--Murfreesboro--Franklin, TN, Memphis, TN-MS-AR, Knoxville, TN, Chattanooga, TN-GA and Johnson City, TN.
