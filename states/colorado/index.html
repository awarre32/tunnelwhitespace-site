Tunnel car washes in Colorado

The map counts 225 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Colorado and 8 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 18 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the Colorado metros with the most room are Denver-Aurora-Centennial, CO, Boulder, CO, Fort Collins-Loveland, CO, Colorado Springs, CO and Greeley, CO.
