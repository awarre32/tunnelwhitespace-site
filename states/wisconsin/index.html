Tunnel car washes in Wisconsin

The map counts 138 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Wisconsin and 11 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 29 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Wisconsin, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington, MN-WI, Milwaukee-Waukesha, WI, Madison, WI, Green Bay, WI and Racine-Mount Pleasant, WI.
