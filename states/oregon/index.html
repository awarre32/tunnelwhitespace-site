Tunnel car washes in Oregon

The map counts 98 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Oregon and 2 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 35 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Oregon, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro, OR-WA, Salem, OR, Eugene-Springfield, OR, Medford, OR and Coos Bay-North Bend, OR (micro).

Whitespace index (estimate): the weighted population in a metro's tracts that are short of tunnel car washes, divided by 20,000. Dense urban tracts are weighted down and income nudges demand. It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites. Tunnels (operating only; tunnels in development are their own column and count as open in the index) and population are actual counts (tunnels verified October 2026, population Esri 2024 estimates).
