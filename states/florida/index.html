Tunnel car washes in Florida

The map counts 932 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Florida and 60 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 3 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Florida, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL, Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater, FL, Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford, FL, Jacksonville, FL and Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach, FL.
