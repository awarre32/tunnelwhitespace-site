Tunnel car washes in Connecticut

The map counts 148 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Connecticut and 3 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 27 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the Connecticut metros with the most room are Bridgeport-Stamford-Danbury, CT, New Haven, CT, Hartford-West Hartford-East Hartford, CT, Waterbury-Shelton, CT and Norwich-New London-Willimantic, CT.
