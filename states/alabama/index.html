Tunnel car washes in Alabama

The map counts 249 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Alabama and none reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 16 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Alabama, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Birmingham, AL, Columbus, GA-AL, Tuscaloosa, AL, Huntsville, AL and Montgomery, AL.
