Tunnel car washes in Idaho

The map counts 71 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Idaho and 2 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 38 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the Idaho metros with the most room are Boise City, ID, Coeur d'Alene, ID and Twin Falls, ID.
