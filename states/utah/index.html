Tunnel car washes in Utah

The map counts 189 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Utah and 3 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 24 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Utah, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Salt Lake City-Murray, UT, Provo-Orem-Lehi, UT, St. George, UT and Ogden, UT.
