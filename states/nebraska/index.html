Tunnel car washes in Nebraska

The map counts 86 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Nebraska and 8 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 36 (tied) of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Nebraska, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Omaha, NE-IA, Lincoln, NE and Norfolk, NE (micro).
