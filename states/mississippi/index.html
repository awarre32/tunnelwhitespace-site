Tunnel car washes in Mississippi

The map counts 105 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Mississippi and none reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 34 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Mississippi, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Memphis, TN-MS-AR and Jackson, MS.

It ranks markets to study; it is not a count of buildable sites.
