Tunnel car washes in Texas

The map counts 1,534 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Texas and 37 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 1 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Texas, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands, TX, Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington, TX, Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos, TX, San Antonio-New Braunfels, TX and McAllen-Edinburg-Mission, TX.
