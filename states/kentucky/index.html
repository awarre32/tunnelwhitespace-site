Tunnel car washes in Kentucky

The map counts 200 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Kentucky and 3 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 21 (tied) of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Kentucky, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Cincinnati, OH-KY-IN, Louisville/Jefferson County, KY-IN, Lexington-Fayette, KY, Clarksville, TN-KY and Huntington-Ashland, WV-KY-OH.
