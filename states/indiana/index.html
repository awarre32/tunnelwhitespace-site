Tunnel car washes in Indiana

The map counts 282 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Indiana and 4 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 14 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Indiana, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Chicago-Naperville-Elgin, IL-IN, Cincinnati, OH-KY-IN, Indianapolis-Carmel-Greenwood, IN, Louisville/Jefferson County, KY-IN and South Bend-Mishawaka, IN-MI.
