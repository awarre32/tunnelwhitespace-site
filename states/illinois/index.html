Tunnel car washes in Illinois

The map counts 470 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Illinois and 13 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 5 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Illinois, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Chicago-Naperville-Elgin, IL-IN, St. Louis, MO-IL, Rockford, IL, Davenport-Moline-Rock Island, IA-IL and Champaign-Urbana, IL.
