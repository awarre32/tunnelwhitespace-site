Tunnel car washes in South Carolina

The map counts 269 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in South Carolina and 4 reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 15 of 51 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

By whitespace index at one tunnel per 20,000 people, the South Carolina metros with the most room are Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia, NC-SC, Columbia, SC, Charleston-North Charleston, SC, Greenville-Anderson-Greer, SC and Augusta-Richmond County, GA-SC.
