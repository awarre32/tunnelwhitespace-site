Tunnel car washes in Kansas

The map counts 115 operating tunnel car washes (express, full-serve and flex) in Kansas and none reported in development, verified October 2026. That is number 33 of the 50 states and DC by operating tunnels. Updated November 25, 2026.

Among the metro and micro areas that include part of Kansas, the ones with the most room by whitespace index (one tunnel per 20,000 people, counted across each whole metro) are Kansas City, MO-KS, Wichita, KS, Lawrence, KS, St. Joseph, MO-KS and Topeka, KS.
